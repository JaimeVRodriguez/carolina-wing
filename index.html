The Championship

One day. Every flavor.

The Carolina Wing Championships (CWC) is a one-day food and music festival bringing together local and regional restaurants, food vendors, businesses, families and wing lovers from across the Carolinas.

CWC celebrates great food, live music, community and a shared passion for supporting Friends of the Skateparks Foundation and the future of Rowan Skate Park.

Zero Footprint Commitment

Reducing event waste through responsible serving practices and eliminating single-use plastics where practical.

Community impact

Fun with a purpose

The Carolina Wing Championships directly supports Friends of the Skateparks Foundation and the future of Rowan Skate Park. Funds raised help with park improvements, including our Lights for Rowan campaign, so the park can be enjoyed safely, for more hours, by everyone in the community.
