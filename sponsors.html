    Sponsor recognition & event branding

    Get seen on everything

    Championship belt

    Only the People's Choice winner receives the custom Carolina Wing Championship belt, plus $750. It goes to the team collecting the most wing-sample tickets and raising the most money for our cause. The belt features the CWC logo in the center, the Presenting Sponsor logo on one side plate and the FSF commemorative coin on the other — a great photo opportunity and lasting sponsor recognition.

    Image: championship belt

    Official event merchandise

    Sponsors gain visibility through high-quality event merchandise including t-shirts, tumblers, hats and more. Presenting, Championship and Diamond sponsors have their logos featured on the official event t-shirt.
